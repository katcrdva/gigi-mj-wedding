Our Story

Our Story

Their beautiful story began in 2018, when Gillan joined a shipping company as an apprentice and met Merry Jane, who was also employed there. What began as a casual encounter gradually developed into a friendship. Talking, laughing, and sharing little moments, they started to get to know one another, which brought their hearts closer and slowly turned into a love that neither of them expected.

On October 20, 2019, they officially became a couple—and began a journey they would never forget. Through the years, they shared countless happy memories and faced their fair share of challenges. Even when distance kept them apart, they remained close at heart and continued to choose each other, day after day.

In 2025, their love took another beautiful step as they got engaged. And now, in 2026, they finally stand together, ready to begin their greatest journey yet—not just as two people in love, but as one family, one future, and one forever.

From the moment they met in 2018 to this beautiful day, their love has grown through every season. Today, Gillan and Merry Jane are not just celebrating the love that brought them here—they are celebrating the forever they are choosing together. ❤️
